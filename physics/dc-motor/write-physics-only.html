All AQA 4.7.2.1 (Physics only) Draft for Alaa's review

A relay. Circuit 1: a cell, a switch and a coil wound on an iron core. Above the coil, an iron armature is pivoted at one end and held up by a spring. Circuit 2: a cell and a motor (M), with a gap between two contacts just under the free end of the armature.

Q9 · ExplainThe diagram shows a relay. Closing the switch in circuit 1 turns on the motor in circuit 2. Explain how. [4 marks]

Show the mark scheme
  • A current flows in the coil (1)
  • The coil becomes an electromagnet (magnetised) (1)
  • It attracts the iron armature (1)
  • The armature pushes the contacts together, completing circuit 2 (1)

Examiner tip: follow the chain from the switch to the motor; each link is a mark.

HT AQA 4.7.2.4 (Physics only) Draft for Alaa's review

Q10 · ExplainExplain how a moving-coil loudspeaker produces a sound from an alternating current. [4 marks]

Show the mark scheme
  • The current in the coil is in a magnetic field, so there is a force on the coil (the motor effect) (1)
  • The current keeps reversing, so the force keeps reversing (1)
  • The coil and cone move back and forth (vibrate) (1)
  • The cone makes pressure variations in the air: a sound wave (1)

Examiner tip: "the magnet vibrates" scores nothing: the magnet stays still; the coil moves.
