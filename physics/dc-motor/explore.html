    Try these All AQA 4.7.2.1
    1. Slide the current from +3 to −3. What happens to the compasses?
    2. Compare the inner and outer compasses. Where is the field stronger?
    3. Make a solenoid, then add the iron core. Where are the poles?

    Try these HT AQA 4.7.2.2, 4.7.2.3
    1. Turn up the current, then add turns. What happens to the speed?
    2. Reverse the current. Then flip the magnets as well.
    3. Switch off the commutator. Where does the coil end up?

    Foundation: this is optional. It shows your electromagnet idea in action.
