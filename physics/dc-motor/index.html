Unit 1 · About 45 minutes

How a DC motor works

  • Physics
  • GCSE

AQA GCSE Physics (8463), 4.7.2 The motor effect (Paper 2). 4.7.2.1 Electromagnetism is for everyone. 4.7.2.2 Fleming's left-hand rule and 4.7.2.3 Electric motors are Higher tier only, marked HT. Specification references for other exam boards are still to be confirmed.

About this unit

What you'll learn

  • All Describe and draw the magnetic field around a wire and a solenoid, and explain how to make it stronger.
  • HT Explain the motor effect and use Fleming's left-hand rule.
  • HT Use F = B I l.
  • HT Explain why the coil of a DC motor turns, and why it keeps turning.
You'll need
Magnetic fields between magnets; current in a circuit.
Key words
Magnetic field, solenoid, electromagnet; HT: motor effect, magnetic flux density, Fleming's left-hand rule, F = B I l, split-ring commutator, brushes.
Where you'll see it
Electric toothbrushes, drones, car windows, computer fans, scrapyard cranes.
