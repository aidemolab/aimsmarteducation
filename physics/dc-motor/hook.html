Warm-up: what do you remember? KS3 recap

Answer in your head, then check.

  1. Which way do magnetic field lines point between two magnets?

    Show the answer

    From the north pole to the south pole.

  2. What is the unit of current?

    Show the answer

    The ampere (A).

  3. What makes a current flow in a circuit?

    Show the answer

    A potential difference, for example from a cell or a power supply.
