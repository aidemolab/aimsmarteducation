All AQA 4.7.2.1 (Physics only) Question 1

You press the switch. What does the electromagnet do to the iron armature?

All AQA 4.7.2.1 (Physics only) Question 2

As the armature moves, the electromagnet switches off. Why?

All AQA 4.7.2.1 (Physics only) Question 3

Explain why the bell keeps ringing for as long as you hold the switch down.

Show the answer

With the electromagnet off, the spring pulls the armature back. It touches the contact screw again, so the circuit is complete and the electromagnet attracts the armature again. This repeats many times a second, so the hammer keeps striking the gong.
