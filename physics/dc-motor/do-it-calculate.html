HT AQA 4.7.2.2 Equations and units

Force on a wire in a magnetic field

F = B I l

force = magnetic flux density × current × length. The wire must be at right angles to the field. A wire parallel to the field feels no force.

Quantities in F = B I l, their symbols and units
QuantitySymbolUnit
ForceFnewton (N)
Magnetic flux densityBtesla (T)
CurrentIampere (A)
Length of wire in the fieldlmetre (m)

Rearranging: B = F ÷ (I l) · I = F ÷ (B l) · l = F ÷ (B I)

Units first: change centimetres to metres (÷ 100), millimetres to metres (÷ 1000) and millinewtons to newtons (÷ 1000) before you substitute.
