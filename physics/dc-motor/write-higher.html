HT Motor exam questions

HT AQA 4.7.2.3

Q5 · StateGive two ways to reverse the direction of rotation of a DC motor. [2 marks]

Show the mark scheme
  • Reverse the direction of the current (swap the supply connections) (1)
  • Reverse the magnetic field (swap the poles of the magnets) (1)

Examiner tip: doing both at once does not reverse it. This is a common wrong answer.

HT AQA 4.7.2.2

Q6 · CalculateA 12 cm length of wire at right angles to a magnetic field of flux density 0.25 T feels a force of 0.090 N. Calculate the current in the wire. [3 marks]

A
Show the mark scheme
  • Converts length: l = 0.12 m (1)
  • Rearranges and substitutes: I = 0.090 ÷ (0.25 × 0.12) (1)
  • I = 3.0 A (1)

Examiner tip: most lost marks come from leaving the length in centimetres.
