HT AQA 4.7.2.2 L5 · Partly worked

8.0 cm of wire carries 2.0 A at right angles to a 0.15 T field. Calculate the force.

Step 1 (done for you): l = 8.0 cm = 0.080 m

Step 2: F = B I l = 0.15 × 2.0 × 0.080

  1. L8HT AQA 4.7.2.4 (Physics only) A loudspeaker has a coil in a magnetic field, attached to a cone. Explain why the cone moves back and forth when an alternating current flows. Going further

    Show the answer

    The current in the coil, in a magnetic field, gives a force (the motor effect). The current keeps reversing, so the force keeps reversing, and the cone moves back and forth.

    Loudspeakers are AQA 4.7.2.4: Physics only, Higher tier.
