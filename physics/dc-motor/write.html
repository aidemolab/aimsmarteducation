Exam-style questions All

All AQA 4.7.2.1

Q1 · DescribeDescribe the magnetic field pattern around a long straight wire carrying a current. [2 marks]

Show the mark scheme
  • Circles (concentric) around the wire (1)
  • Field weaker / lines further apart further from the wire (1)

Examiner tip: draw circles centred on the wire, not lines spreading out from it.

All AQA 4.7.2.1

Q2 · GiveGive two ways to increase the strength of the magnetic field of a solenoid. [2 marks]

Show the mark scheme
  • Any two from: increase the current (1); add an iron core (1); more turns (in the same length) (1)

Examiner tip: "reverse the current" scores nothing: it swaps the poles but does not change the strength.

All AQA 4.7.2.1

Q3 · DescribeDescribe the magnetic field inside and outside a solenoid carrying a current. [3 marks] ✦ AI Exam Coach

2 of 3 marking points

  • ✓ The field inside is strong.
  • ✗ Not yet awarded. Missing: what the field inside is like (its pattern).
  • ✓ Outside, the same shape as a bar magnet's field.

Hint: how are the field lines spaced inside the solenoid?

Example feedback. The live coach will mark against the teacher's own mark scheme and give hints, never the full answer.
