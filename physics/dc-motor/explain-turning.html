HT How the motor works: five steps

HT AQA 4.7.2.3 Step 4

The coil turns

The forces on the two sides act in opposite directions, so together they turn the coil.

Quick check: why do the two forces make the coil turn?

Show the answer

They act in opposite directions on opposite sides, giving a turning effect.

Pause and explain ✦ AI tutor

  • AI tutor: Why do the two sides of the coil move in opposite directions?
  1. Hint 1Trace the current around the coil.
  2. Hint 2Which way does it flow in each side?
  3. Model pointsThe current flows in opposite directions in the two sides, so the forces on them are opposite.

Analogy: a stream Draft for Alaa's review

The coil is like a paddle wheel in the stream: the flow pushes up on the paddle on one side and down on the other, so the wheel turns. The commutator is like turning the paddles round every half turn, so the stream keeps pushing the wheel the same way.

HT AQA 4.7.2.3 Step 5 Going further

The commutator keeps it turning

Every half turn, the split-ring commutator reverses the current in the coil. That keeps the forces turning the coil the same way.

Quick check: what does the commutator do every half turn?

Show the answer

It reverses the direction of the current in the coil.

The AQA specification asks you to explain why the coil rotates, but does not name the commutator.

Pause and explain ✦ AI tutor Going further

  2. Hint 2Which way do they now turn the coil?
  3. Model pointsAfter the vertical, the forces would turn the coil back the other way, so it rocks and settles at the vertical.
