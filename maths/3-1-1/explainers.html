Explainers

Lessons N1 to N9: 47 explainers, one goal each, on two pages. HT = Higher tier only.

N1 Ordering numbers and using inequality symbols

3 explainers: N1.1 to N1.3

  • N1.1Order positive and negative integers by their position on a number line.
  • N1.2Order positive and negative decimals and fractions by placing them on one common scale.
  • N1.3Choose the symbol from =, ≠, <, >, ≤, ≥ that makes a number statement true.

N2 The four operations and place value

12 explainers: N2.1 to N2.12

  • N2.1Identify the value of a digit from its place in a whole number or decimal.
  • N2.2Multiply or divide a number by a power of ten by moving its digits between place-value columns.
  • N2.3Add or subtract integers and decimals with a written column method by lining up place values.
  • N2.4Multiply integers and decimals using a written method built from partial products.
  • N2.5Divide integers and decimals using a written division method.
  • N2.6Add or subtract negative numbers by reading direction on a number line.
  • N2.7Decide the sign of a product or quotient when negative numbers are involved.
  • N2.8Add or subtract fractions and mixed numbers by first making equal-sized parts.
  • N2.9Multiply fractions and mixed numbers, changing mixed numbers to improper fractions first.
  • N2.10Divide by a fraction by finding how many divisor-sized groups fit.
  • N2.11Choose the calculation that matches profit, loss, cost price, selling price, debit, credit or balance in a money problem.
  • N2.12Interpret income tax, VAT and interest rate amounts given in a household money problem.

N3 Order of operations and inverse operations

4 explainers: N3.1 to N3.4

  • N3.1Evaluate an expression using the conventional priority of operations.
  • N3.2Use inverse operations to undo a calculation and check an answer.
  • N3.3Find the reciprocal of a non-zero number.
  • N3.4Simplify a calculation by cancelling a common factor where it is valid.

N4 Primes, factors, multiples, HCF and LCM

5 explainers: N4.1 to N4.5

  • N4.1Tell a factor from a multiple using equal groups.
  • N4.2Identify a prime number by its positive factors.
  • N4.3Write a whole number greater than 1 as a product of prime factors in index form.
  • N4.4Find the highest common factor of two or more numbers.
  • N4.5Find the lowest common multiple of two or more numbers.
