Q1 · Drag and drop into blanks (open answer: build the grid on paper, then type the count)

Spinner A shows 1, 2 or 3. Spinner B shows 1, 2, 3 or 4. Make a grid of totals with one row for each number on A and one column for each number on B. How many outcomes give a total of 5?

Choose your tool first (scale, tiles or grid) and say why, then do the task. Afterwards compare with the worked route in the feedback.

Draw the grid on paper and fill in every total, then type your count.

Q2 · Matching (shown as multiple choice: pick the right matching)

A shop buys a jacket for £36.40 and sells it for £52.00. Match each word to its amount: cost price, selling price, profit. Amounts: £36.40, £52.00, £15.60

Choose your tool first (scale, tiles or grid) and say why, then do the task. Afterwards compare with the worked route in the feedback.

Q3 · Sort into groups (shown as multiple choice: pick the group that is exactly “in standard form”)

Sort these measurements (in metres) into “in standard form” and “not in standard form”: 4.5 × 10⁷, 45 × 10⁶, 0.45 × 10⁸, 1 × 10⁻³, 9.99 × 10⁰, 10 × 10². Which list is exactly the “in standard form” group?

Choose your tool first (scale, tiles or grid) and say why, then do the task. Afterwards compare with the worked route in the feedback.
