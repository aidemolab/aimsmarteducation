Q10 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: pick the answers to (a) and (b)) · Non-calculator · 3 marks

Answer both parts.

(a) Work out 46 × 35. Show your method. [2]

(b) Use your answer to (a) to write down the value of 4.6 × 0.35. [1]

Mark scheme

(a) M1 for a complete method with partial products, for example 1380 and 230 seen; A1 for 1610.

(b) B1 for 1.61.

Q11 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: the answer is a clock time) · Non-calculator · 3 marks

Bus A leaves a bus station every 6 minutes. Bus B leaves every 15 minutes. Both buses leave together at 9:00 am. At what time do they next leave together?

Mark scheme

M1 for 6 = 2 × 3 and 15 = 3 × 5, or lists of multiples of 6 and 15.

A1 for LCM 30.

A1 for 9:30 am.

HT Q12 · Numeric answer (open answer: type your estimate to 1 decimal place; 9.1 to 9.2 is accepted) · Non-calculator · 2 marks

Estimate the value of √83. Give your answer to 1 decimal place.

Higher tier only. Foundation students can skip this part: Q20 is your question for this N point.

Mark scheme

M1 for 9² = 81 and 10² = 100 seen, or “between 9 and 10”.

A1 for an answer from 9.1 to 9.2.
