Warm-up: spacing from N7 (index laws)

Q1 · Multiple choice · Spacing: N7 (index laws)

Write 10⁵ × 10³ as a single power of 10.

Hint 1

10⁵ means five tens multiplied together, and 10³ means three more tens multiplied.

Hint 2

How many tens are multiplied together altogether?

Goal N9.1: Convert a number between ordinary form and standard form

Worked example (skip it if you're confident)

Write 36 000 in standard form.

Put the decimal point after the first non-zero digit: 3.6, which is between 1 and 10.

The digits move 4 columns from 3.6 to 36 000, so the power is 4.

Answer: 3.6 × 10⁴.

Q2 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: the answer is in standard form)

Write 72 000 in standard form.

Hint 1

Put the decimal point straight after the first non-zero digit. What is A?

Hint 2

A is 7.2. Count how many columns the digits move to get from 7.2 back to 72 000.

Hint 3

The digits move 4 columns, so you multiply by 10 four times.

Q3 · Multiple choice

Which of these is 0.0049 written in standard form?

Hint 1

The number is less than 1, so will the power be positive or negative?

Hint 2

A is 4.9. To get from 4.9 to 0.0049, the digits move to the right. How many columns?
