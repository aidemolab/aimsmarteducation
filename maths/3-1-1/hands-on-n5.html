Goal N5.1: List all possible outcomes by holding one choice fixed at a time

Q1 · Drag and drop into blanks (shown as a numeric answer: build the grid on paper, then type the total)

A set menu has one starter, one main and one dessert. Starters: soup (S) or melon (M). Mains: pie (P), curry (C) or fish (F). Desserts: ice cream (I) or jelly (J). Build every meal, holding the starter fixed first and then the main. How many meals are there?

Q2 · Numeric answer

Ama has three digit cards: 2, 5 and 5. She uses all three cards to make three-digit numbers. How many different numbers can she make?

Goal N5.2: Check that a list, table or diagram is complete and counts no outcome twice

Q3 · Spot the error in a worked solution

Two spinners are each numbered 1, 2 and 3. Both are spun and the two scores are added. Kai writes: “The totals can be 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6, so there are 5 outcomes.” Build the two-way table of totals on paper. What is Kai's error?
