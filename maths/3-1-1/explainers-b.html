N5 Systematic listing and the product rule for counting

3 explainers: N5.1 to N5.3

  • N5.1List all possible outcomes by holding one choice fixed at a time.
  • N5.2Check that a list, table or diagram is complete and counts no outcome twice.
  • N5.3 HTCount combined choices using the product rule.

N6 Powers and roots

4 explainers: N6.1 to N6.4

  • N6.1Evaluate a positive integer power as repeated multiplication.
  • N6.2Recognise the powers of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10 and the square numbers up to 15 × 15.
  • N6.3Find an exact real root by reversing a known power.
  • N6.4 HTEstimate a power or root of a positive number using the nearest known values.

N7 Calculating with roots and indices

5 explainers: N7.1 to N7.5

  • N7.1Calculate an expression that contains exact numerical roots.
  • N7.2Simplify products and quotients of powers of the same base.
  • N7.3Simplify a power raised to another integer power.
  • N7.4Evaluate a power with a zero or negative integer index for a non-zero base.
  • N7.5 HTEvaluate a fractional power by reading its denominator as a root and its numerator as a power.

N8 Exact calculation: fractions, multiples of π and surds

7 explainers: N8.1 to N8.7

  • N8.1Keep a fraction calculation exact through to its final step.
  • N8.2Calculate exactly with multiples of π by keeping π as a symbol.
  • N8.3 HTSimplify a surd by taking out its largest square factor.
  • N8.4 HTAdd or subtract surds by collecting like surd terms.
  • N8.5 HTMultiply or divide surds exactly.
  • N8.6 HTRationalise a denominator that is a single surd.
  • N8.7 HTRationalise a denominator that has two terms.

N9 Standard form

4 explainers: N9.1 to N9.4

  • N9.1Convert a number between ordinary form and standard form.
  • N9.2Interpret a calculator's standard-form display.
  • N9.3Multiply or divide numbers in standard form.
  • N9.4Add or subtract numbers in standard form by first matching their powers of ten.
