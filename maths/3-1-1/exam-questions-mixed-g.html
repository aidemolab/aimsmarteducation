Q19 · Numeric answer · Non-calculator · 3 marks

Work out √49 × 2³ − 4⁰.

Foundation replacement for Q6 (same N point).

Mark scheme

B1 for √49 = 7 and 2³ = 8.

B1 for 4⁰ = 1.

B1 for 55.

Q20 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: pick the complete list) · Non-calculator · 2 marks

Write down all the square numbers between 100 and 230.

Foundation replacement for Q12 (same N point).

Mark scheme

B1 for at least 3 correct with none wrong.

B1 for all 5 correct with none wrong.

Q21 · Numeric answer · Non-calculator · 3 marks

Work out 2 2/3 × 1 1/8. Give your answer in its simplest form.

Foundation replacement for Q13 (same N point).

Mark scheme

M1 for 8/3 and 9/8.

M1 for 72/24 or a correct cancelled product.

A1 for 3.
