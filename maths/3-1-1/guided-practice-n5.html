Goal N5.1: List all possible outcomes by holding one choice fixed at a time

Worked example (skip it if you're confident)

A café offers toast on white or brown bread, with butter, jam or honey.

Hold white fixed: white with butter, white with jam, white with honey.

Hold brown fixed: brown with butter, brown with jam, brown with honey.

Two rows of three: 3 + 3 = 6 outcomes.

Q1 · Fill in the missing step

A pizza has a thin (T) or deep (D) base, and one topping: cheese (C), mushroom (M) or pepper (P). Leah holds the base fixed and lists: TC, TM, ____, DC, DM, DP. Which outcome fills the gap?

Hint 1

Which base is held fixed in the first row?

Hint 2

Which topping has not yet been paired with T?

Q2 · Ordering or sequencing (shown as multiple choice: pick the systematic order)

Jo throws a coin (H or T) and spins a spinner numbered 1, 2, 3. She writes the outcomes on cards in a random order: T2, H1, T1, H3, H2, T3. Which order is systematic, holding the coin fixed first?

Hint 1

Start with all the cards that begin with H.

Hint 2

Inside each row, put the spinner numbers in order, 1 then 2 then 3.

Q3 · Numeric answer · no hints

List all the ways to arrange the letters A, B and C in a row, using each letter once. How many arrangements are there?
