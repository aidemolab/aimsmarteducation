Q1 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: pick the answers to (a) and (b)) · Calculator allowed · 3 marks

A flower bed is a semicircle with radius 10 m.

(a) Work out the area of the flower bed. Give your answer in terms of π. [2]

(b) Ben says, “The area is 157.1 m², and that is exact.” Is Ben right? Give a reason. [1]

Mark scheme

(a) M1 for π × 10² ÷ 2 (or 100π ÷ 2); A1 for 50π.

(b) B1 for “No” with a reason that 157.1 is rounded or approximate.

Q2 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: pick the answers to (a) and (b)) · Non-calculator · 3 marks

Answer both parts.

(a) Write down the reciprocal of 0.25. [1]

(b) Work out 4 + 6² ÷ (5 − 2). [2]

Mark scheme

(a) B1 for 4.

(b) M1 for 36 ÷ 3 or 12 seen; A1 for 16.

Q3 · Open-ended (shown as multiple choice: pick the complete list; write your method on paper) · Non-calculator · 3 marks

Maya has four coins: 10p, 20p, 50p and £1. She picks two different coins.

List all the possible totals she could make. Show how you know you have found them all.

Mark scheme

B1 for a systematic method (for example, holding one coin fixed at a time, or a table).

B1 for at least 4 correct totals with no repeats.

B1 for all 6 correct totals, with no repeats and no extras.
