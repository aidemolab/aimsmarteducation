Exam-style questions: N9 only

Original questions in the style of an AQA GCSE paper, never copied from AQA papers: 9 questions, 19 marks. M marks are for a correct method, A marks for a correct answer after a correct method, B marks for a correct answer or statement on its own. Open the mark scheme after you answer.

Q1 · Multiple choice · Non-calculator · [1 mark]

Write 0.00083 in standard form. Circle your answer.

Mark scheme

B1 for 8.3 × 10⁻⁴.

Q2 · Ordering or sequencing (shown as multiple choice: pick the correct order) · Non-calculator · [2 marks]

Write these numbers in order of size. Start with the smallest. 6.1 × 10⁻³ · 6.05 × 10⁻² · 6.2 × 10⁻⁴ · 0.006

Mark scheme

B2 for all four in the correct order.

B1 for three of the four in the correct relative order, or for all four correctly written as ordinary numbers (0.0061, 0.0605, 0.00062, 0.006) or all four correctly in standard form (0.006 = 6 × 10⁻³).

Q3 · Numeric answer · Calculator allowed · [1 mark]

Priya works out 0.0000176 × 0.4 on her calculator. The display shows 7.04E−06. Write the answer as an ordinary number.

Mark scheme

B1 for 0.00000704 (accept 7.04 × 10⁻⁶ only if the question asked for standard form; here it asks for an ordinary number).
