Q7 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: pick the answers to (a) and (b)) · Non-calculator · 4 marks

A teacher has 90 red counters and 126 blue counters. She puts all of them into identical bags, with no counters left over. Every bag holds the same mix of red and blue.

(a) What is the greatest number of bags she can fill? [3]

(b) How many red and how many blue counters go in each bag? [1]

Mark scheme

(a) M1 for 90 = 2 × 3² × 5 or 126 = 2 × 3² × 7 (or a full list of factors); M1 for both, or for the common primes 2 × 3² identified; A1 for 18.

(b) B1 for 5 red and 7 blue.

Q8 · Numeric answer · Non-calculator · 3 marks

A television costs £420 plus VAT. The VAT is £84. Priya pays a deposit of £150. She pays the rest in 6 equal monthly payments. Work out the amount of each monthly payment.

Type the amount in pounds, without the £ sign.

Mark scheme

M1 for 420 + 84 = 504.

M1 for (504 − 150) ÷ 6, or 354 seen.

A1 for £59.

Q9 · True or false with a reason (shown as multiple choice: pick the verdicts; write the reasons on paper) · Non-calculator · 2 marks

For each statement, write true or false. Give a reason for each.

A (−5)³ = −125

B 2⁶ = 12

Mark scheme

B1 for A true with a reason.

B1 for B false with 64 (or the repeated multiplication) shown.
