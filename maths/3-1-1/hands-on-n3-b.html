Q5 · Numeric answer

A number is divided by 3. Then 8 is subtracted. Then the result is squared. The final answer is 4. Just before squaring, the number was positive. Find the starting number.

Q6 · True or false with a reason

Leo says 2/3 ÷ 4/9 = 8/27. Use an inverse operation to decide whether he is right.

Q7 · Matching (shown as multiple choice: pick the list where every pair is right)

Which list matches each number to its reciprocal?

Q8 · Drag and drop into blanks (shown as multiple choice: pick the numbers for the blanks, in order)

Work out (45 × 28) ÷ (21 × 15) by cancelling. Which numbers fill the blanks?

45 and 15 share a factor of 15: 45 becomes ▢ and 15 becomes 1.
28 and 21 share a factor of 7: 28 becomes ▢ and 21 becomes ▢.
The calculation becomes (3 × 4) ÷ (3 × 1) = ▢.

Q9 · Spacing (N1) · Ordering or sequencing (shown as multiple choice: pick the right order)

Work out each value, then pick the order from smallest to largest: −3², 2 − 7, 1/4, 0.3

Q10 · Spacing (N2) · Numeric answer

A shop buys a coat for £35 (the cost price) and sells it for £52 (the selling price). Work out the profit in pounds, then check your answer with an inverse operation.
