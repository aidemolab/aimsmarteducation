Goal N3.1: Evaluate an expression using the conventional priority of operations

Worked example (skip it if you're confident)

Work out 5 + 6 × 2. Multiplication comes before addition, because 6 × 2 is one bundle of six twos. 6 × 2 = 12. Then 5 + 12 = 17.

Q1 · Multiple choice

Work out 8 + 2 × 6.

Hint 1

Look for any multiplication or division. That part comes before the adding.

Hint 2

Work out 2 × 6 first.

Hint 3

2 × 6 = 12. Now add the 8.

Q2 · Fill in the missing step

Fill in the missing number.

18 − 12 ÷ 3 × 2
= 18 − ▢ × 2
= 18 − 8
= 10

Hint 1

Division and multiplication have equal priority, so work from left to right.

Hint 2

The first step on the left is 12 ÷ 3.

Q3 · Numeric answer · no hints

Work out 30 − (2 + 3)² ÷ 5.
