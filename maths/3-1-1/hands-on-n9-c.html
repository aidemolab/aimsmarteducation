Q6 · Drag and drop into blanks (shown as multiple choice: pick the cards for the blanks)

Work out (1.8 × 10⁻⁴) ÷ (6 × 10³). Cards: 0.3 · 3 · −7 · −1 · −8 · −6 · 1. Which cards fill the blanks? (1.8 ÷ 6) × 10^(−4 − 3) = ____ × 10^____ = ____ × 10^____

Goal N9.4: Add or subtract by first matching the powers of ten

Q7 · Matching (shown as multiple choice: pick the correct matching)

Match each calculation to its answer. Calculations: (a) 5 × 10³ + 4 × 10² · (b) 5 × 10³ − 4 × 10² · (c) 5 × 10⁻³ + 4 × 10⁻² · (d) 5 × 10³ + 4 × 10³. Answers: 9 × 10³ · 4.5 × 10⁻² · 5.4 × 10³ · 4.6 × 10³

Q8 (part 1) · Spot the error in a worked solution

Ali works out 7.5 × 10⁶ − 9 × 10⁵. Line 1: 7.5 × 10⁶ = 0.75 × 10⁵. Line 2: 0.75 × 10⁵ − 9 × 10⁵ = −8.25 × 10⁵. Which is the first line with an error?
