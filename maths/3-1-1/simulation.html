Simulation

One simulation for the whole unit: predict what will happen, then test it and check.

The Zoomable Number Scale

Goal: write the target number in standard form, A × 10ⁿ with 1 ≤ A < 10, without changing its value. The target pin never moves.

Your cards: 35 × 10⁴ = 350 000

A number scale with a target pin and your pin.

Scale from 10³ to 10⁷; each step to the right is ten times bigger. Target pin at 350 000. Your pin is on the target.

Predict first: if you divide the coefficient 35 by 10 and keep 10⁴, where will your pin land? Choose below or drag the dashed pin on the scale.

This model goes with the N9 explainers on standard form. Watch Explainer N9 for the full explanation.
