Q9 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: the answer is a power)

Write 5⁸ ÷ 5³ × 5 as a single power of 5.

Hint (open it only after you have chosen an approach)

Write each power as a row of 5-tiles, join rows when you multiply and cancel matching tiles when you divide.
