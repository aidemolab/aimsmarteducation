Q4 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: the answer is in standard form) · Non-calculator · 3 marks

Work out (3 × 10⁻⁴) ÷ (7.5 × 10⁻¹⁰). Give your answer in standard form.

Mark scheme

M1 for 3 ÷ 7.5 = 0.4 or 10⁻⁴ ÷ 10⁻¹⁰ = 10⁶.

M1 for 0.4 × 10⁶ (or 400 000).

A1 for 4 × 10⁵.

Q5 · Ordering or sequencing (shown as multiple choice: pick the right order) · Non-calculator · 2 marks

Write these numbers in order of size. Start with the smallest: −0.6, −2/3, −5/8, −0.65

Mark scheme

B1 for −5/8 = −0.625 or −2/3 = −0.666... seen.

B1 for the correct order.

HT Q6 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: the answer is a fraction) · Non-calculator · 2 marks

Work out the value of 32^(−3/5). Give your answer as a fraction.

Higher tier only. Foundation students can skip this part: Q19 is your question for this N point.

Mark scheme

M1 for the fifth root of 32 = 2, or 2³ = 8, or 1/32^(3/5) seen.

A1 for 1/8.
