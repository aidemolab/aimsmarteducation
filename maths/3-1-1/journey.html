The route

This page shows the route through the unit. Follow it in order, or jump straight to any N point: every part below has an "N1 only" to "N9 only" menu, so you can tap the N point you want to work on and land on that section. Open a part to see what you do there.

Intro

you meet Maya and Ben and lock in a prediction: which bank balance is greater, −£8 or −£3? You find out in the first N1 video (N1.1), and you test it again in the Simulation's warm-up round. Jump to any N point from the menu at the bottom of the Intro. Go to Intro

Learning Journey (this page)

you see what you will be able to do at each N point. Tap an N point below to open its explainers.

Explainers

you watch one short AIM Narrator video per goal and do its two pause tasks. Pick an N point from the menu to start at its first video. Go to Explainers

Simulation

you rewrite a number in standard form on the Zoomable Number Scale without moving its pin, predicting before each change. It uses N1, N2, N7 and N9; the menu links back to those explainers. Go to Simulation

Guided Practice

in each "N1 only" to "N9 only" section you study a worked example, then try a similar question, and the support fades until you finish with a question on your own (if you are confident, you can skip the worked examples). In the mixed set there is no worked example: the questions jump between N points, so you choose the method yourself, a hint appears only after you have chosen, and on the second visit to each N point there is no hint. Choose one N section to target one point, or the mixed set to practise them all. Go to Guided Practice

Hands-on practice

you move, sort and build with the scale, tiles and grids before the reason is revealed. In the mixed set you choose a tool first, say why, and then compare your route with the worked route shown in the feedback. Choose any "N only" section or the mixed set. Go to Hands-on practice

Exam-style questions

you answer original AQA-style questions with marks shown, then read mark-scheme feedback. Choose any "N only" section or the mixed set. Go to Exam-style questions

Recap

you read one line per N point and check your confidence; if you are not confident, open that N point's "Not confident?" line to see its help links. Go to Recap

Survey

you think back over what you liked, what you disliked and what could be better. Answers are not collected yet, so nothing is sent. Go to Survey

What you will be able to do

HT = Higher tier only. Foundation students can skip HT parts; every other part is for everyone. Open an N point to see its goals; each goal links to its explainer.

N1 Ordering numbers and using inequality symbols

Order negative numbers, decimals and fractions and choose the right symbol.

  • N1.1 Order positive and negative integers by their position on a number line.
  • N1.2 Order positive and negative decimals and fractions by placing them on one common scale.
  • N1.3 Choose the symbol from =, ≠, <, >, ≤, ≥ that makes a number statement true.
N2 The four operations and place value

Choose and carry out the right operation with negatives, decimals, fractions and money terms.

  • N2.1 Identify the value of a digit from its place in a whole number or decimal.
  • N2.2 Multiply or divide a number by a power of ten by moving its digits between place-value columns.
  • N2.3 Add or subtract integers and decimals with a written column method by lining up place values.
  • N2.4 Multiply integers and decimals using a written method built from partial products.
  • N2.5 Divide integers and decimals using a written division method.
  • N2.6 Add or subtract negative numbers by reading direction on a number line.
  • N2.7 Decide the sign of a product or quotient when negative numbers are involved.
  • N2.8 Add or subtract fractions and mixed numbers by first making equal-sized parts.
  • N2.9 Multiply fractions and mixed numbers, changing mixed numbers to improper fractions first.
  • N2.10 Divide by a fraction by finding how many divisor-sized groups fit.
  • N2.11 Choose the calculation that matches profit, loss, cost price, selling price, debit, credit or balance in a money problem.
  • N2.12 Interpret income tax, VAT and interest rate amounts given in a household money problem.
N3 Order of operations and inverse operations

Tell which step comes first and how to undo it.

  • N3.1 Evaluate an expression using the conventional priority of operations.
  • N3.2 Use inverse operations to undo a calculation and check an answer.
  • N3.3 Find the reciprocal of a non-zero number.
  • N3.4 Simplify a calculation by cancelling a common factor where it is valid.
N4 Primes, factors, multiples, HCF and LCM

Write a number as a product of primes and decide whether a problem needs the HCF or the LCM.

  • N4.1 Tell a factor from a multiple using equal groups.
  • N4.2 Identify a prime number by its positive factors.
  • N4.3 Write a whole number greater than 1 as a product of prime factors in index form.
  • N4.4 Find the highest common factor of two or more numbers.
  • N4.5 Find the lowest common multiple of two or more numbers.
N5 Systematic listing and the product rule for counting + HT

Show that no outcome is missing or counted twice.

  • N5.1 List all possible outcomes by holding one choice fixed at a time.
  • N5.2 Check that a list, table or diagram is complete and counts no outcome twice.
  • N5.3 HT Count combined choices using the product rule.
N6 Powers and roots + HT

Work out a power and reverse it to find a root.

  • N6.1 Evaluate a positive integer power as repeated multiplication.
  • N6.2 Recognise the powers of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10 and the square numbers up to 15 × 15.
  • N6.3 Find an exact real root by reversing a known power.
  • N6.4 HT Estimate a power or root of a positive number using the nearest known values.
N7 Calculating with roots and indices + HT

Calculate with roots and integer indices.

  • N7.1 Calculate an expression that contains exact numerical roots.
  • N7.2 Simplify products and quotients of powers of the same base.
  • N7.3 Simplify a power raised to another integer power.
  • N7.4 Evaluate a power with a zero or negative integer index for a non-zero base.
  • N7.5 HT Evaluate a fractional power by reading its denominator as a root and its numerator as a power.
N8 Exact calculation: fractions, multiples of π and surds + HT

Keep answers exact.

  • N8.1 Keep a fraction calculation exact through to its final step.
  • N8.2 Calculate exactly with multiples of π by keeping π as a symbol.
  • N8.3 HT Simplify a surd by taking out its largest square factor.
  • N8.4 HT Add or subtract surds by collecting like surd terms.
  • N8.5 HT Multiply or divide surds exactly.
  • N8.6 HT Rationalise a denominator that is a single surd.
  • N8.7 HT Rationalise a denominator that has two terms.
N9 Standard form

Write, read and calculate with numbers in standard form.

  • N9.1 Convert a number between ordinary form and standard form.
  • N9.2 Interpret a calculator's standard-form display.
  • N9.3 Multiply or divide numbers in standard form.
  • N9.4 Add or subtract numbers in standard form by first matching their powers of ten.
