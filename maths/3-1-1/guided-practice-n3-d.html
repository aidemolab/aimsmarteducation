Goal N3.4: Simplify a calculation by cancelling a common factor where it is valid

Worked example (skip it if you're confident)

Work out (16 × 9) ÷ 8. 16 is 2 × 8, so the top is 2 × 8 × 9. Dividing by 8 undoes the × 8, which leaves 2 × 9 = 18. Check the long way: 16 × 9 = 144 and 144 ÷ 8 = 18.

Q10 · Numeric answer

Use cancelling to work out (30 × 7) ÷ 15.

Hint 1

Look for a number on top that 15 divides into exactly.

Hint 2

30 ÷ 15 = 2.

Hint 3

So the calculation becomes 2 × 7.

Q11 · Multiple response (shown as multiple choice: pick the list with every right fraction)

In which of these can you cancel a common factor of 5 from the whole top and the whole bottom?

A (5 × 8)/5    B (5 + 8)/5    C (5 × 3 × 2)/(5 × 7)    D (5 + 10)/5

Hint 1

Ask: does 5 divide the WHOLE top?

Hint 2

For a sum, 5 must divide every term.

Q13 · Spacing (N2: negative numbers) · Multiple choice

Work out −4 × 3 + 20 ÷ −5.

Hint 1

Multiplying and dividing come before adding, so work out −4 × 3 and 20 ÷ −5 first.

Hint 2

A negative times a positive is negative: −4 × 3 = −12.

Hint 3

A positive divided by a negative is negative: 20 ÷ −5 = −4. Now work out −12 + (−4).

Q14 · Spacing (N1: inequality symbols) · Drag and drop into blanks (shown as multiple choice: pick the symbol)

Which symbol makes this true?   2 + 3 × 4  ▢  (2 + 3) × 4

Hint 1

Work out each side on its own first, using the order of operations.

Hint 2

The left side has no brackets, so 3 × 4 comes first. The right side has brackets, so 2 + 3 comes first.
