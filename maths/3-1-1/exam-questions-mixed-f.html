Q16 · True or false with a reason (shown as multiple choice: pick the verdict with the correct value) · Non-calculator · 2 marks

Ben says, “3 + 5 × 2² = 32.” Is Ben right? Give a reason and the correct value.

Mark scheme

B1 for “No” with a reason (Ben added first, or the power and multiplication come before the addition).

B1 for 23.

Q17 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: pick the answers to (a) and (b)) · Non-calculator · 3 marks

Answer both parts.

(a) Write 3.7 × 10⁻⁴ as an ordinary number. [1]

(b) Work out 6 × 10⁵ + 4.5 × 10⁴. Give your answer in standard form. [2]

Mark scheme

(a) B1 for 0.00037.

(b) M1 for 600 000 + 45 000, or 6 × 10⁵ + 0.45 × 10⁵; A1 for 6.45 × 10⁵.

Q18 · Drag and drop into blanks (shown as multiple choice: pick the card for each blank) · Non-calculator · 2 marks

Which card goes in each blank? (a) 7⁵ × 7⁻² = ☐ (b) 2⁻³ = ☐

Cards: 7³, 7⁻¹⁰, 49³, 1/8, −8, −6

Mark scheme

B1 for (a) 7³.

B1 for (b) 1/8.
