Q22 · Open-ended (shown as multiple choice: pick the verdict; write your list on paper) · Non-calculator · 3 marks

A café meal deal is one drink and one snack. The drinks are water (W), juice (J) and milk (M). The snacks are an apple (A) and crisps (C).

Foundation replacement for Q14 (same N point).

Ben says, “There are 5 different meal deals.” Show that Ben is wrong. Explain how you know your list is complete.

Mark scheme

B1 for a systematic list or table (for example, one row for each drink).

B1 for all 6 meal deals with no repeats.

B1 for a statement that there are 6, not 5, so Ben is wrong.
