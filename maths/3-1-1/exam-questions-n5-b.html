Exam-style questions: N5 only

Original questions in the style of an AQA GCSE paper. Command words: list, write down, work out, show that, explain. M marks are for a correct method, A marks for a correct answer after a correct method, B marks for a correct answer or statement on its own.

Q4 · True or false with a reason · [2 marks]

Two fair six-sided dice are rolled, one red and one blue. Tom says, “There are 6 + 6 = 12 possible outcomes.” Is Tom correct? Choose the answer with the reason.

Mark scheme

B1 for “no” (or “false”).

B1 for a correct reason with 36, for example a sample space table with six rows of six cells, or 6 × 6 = 36.

HT Q5 · Numeric answer · [3 marks]

A password is made of 2 capital letters followed by 3 digits. Letters can repeat. The 3 digits must all be different. Work out how many different passwords are possible. Type the number without spaces.

Mark scheme

M1 for 26 × 26 = 676 for the letters.

M1 for 10 × 9 × 8 = 720 for the digits, because each digit used cannot be used again.

A1 for 676 × 720 = 486 720, or 26 × 26 × 10 × 9 × 8 = 486 720 in one product.

HT Q6(a) · Numeric answer · [2 marks]

There are 8 teams in a league. (a) Each team plays every other team twice, once at home and once away. Work out the total number of matches.

Mark scheme

M1 for 8 × 7.

A1 for 56.

HT Q6(b) · Numeric answer · [2 marks]

(b) In a cup, each pair of teams plays only once. Work out the number of cup matches.

Mark scheme

M1 for 56 ÷ 2, because each pair is counted twice in part (a).

A1 for 28.
