Q4 · Drag and drop into blanks (open answer: sort the tiles on paper, then type the number of packs)

A teacher packs 60 pencils and 72 rubbers into identical packs, with nothing left over. Each pack has the same mix. 60 = 2² × 3 × 5 and 72 = 2³ × 3². Put the prime tiles into a two-circle diagram, then fill in: the largest number of packs is ☐.
