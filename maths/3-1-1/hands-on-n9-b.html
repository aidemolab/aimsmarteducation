Q4 (part 1) · Spot the error in a worked solution

Sam works out 5 000 000 × 8 000 000 on a calculator. The display shows 4E13. Sam writes: Line 1: The display means 4¹³. Line 2: 4¹³ = 67 108 864. Line 3: So 5 000 000 × 8 000 000 = 67 108 864. Which is the first line with an error?

Q4 (part 2) · Spot the error in a worked solution

Now write the correct answer to 5 000 000 × 8 000 000 in standard form.

Goal N9.3: Multiply or divide numbers in standard form

Q5 (step 1) · Predict, then check

Work out (7 × 10⁵) × (3 × 10⁻²). Multiplying the coefficients and adding the indices gives the card 21 × 10³, which sits on the target pin (21 000). Predict: if you change the coefficient to 2.1 and keep 10³, where will the pin land?

You can make the change and watch the pin on the unit's simulation.

Q5 (step 2) · Predict, then check

Predict: with the coefficient 2.1, which power of ten brings the pin back to the target, 21 000?
