Unit 1 · Number

Structure and calculation

AQA 8300 · 3.1.1 · N1–N9

By the end you'll be able to order and calculate with any kind of number, use factors, powers and roots, and write very large and very small numbers in standard form.

Intro: Maya and Ben

Maya and Ben, both 15, sit at a kitchen table with two phones showing bank apps, a till receipt and a round cake tin for their charity bake sale.

Ben: My account says −£8. Yours says −£3. So I'm better off, because eight's bigger.

Maya: Are you sure? Lock in your answer. In the very first video we'll put both balances on a number line and see who's right.

Ben: Locked. Next problem: this receipt says 3 + 4 × 2 = 11. I make it 14.

Maya: And the charity says the bake sales raised 2.5 × 10⁶ pounds altogether. What does that even mean?

Ben: So how do we compare, calculate and write any number exactly, from tiny decimals to millions?

Your turn: lock in your answer now. Who is better off, −£8 or −£3? You find out in the first N1 video (N1.1), and you test it again in the Simulation's warm-up round. To jump to any N point, use the N1 to N9 menu in Explainers below.
