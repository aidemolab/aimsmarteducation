Q1 · Ordering or sequencing (shown as multiple choice: pick the right order)

Put these numbers in order, smallest first: 0.3, −0.45, −1/2, 1/4, −0.125

Hint (open it only after you have chosen an approach)

Zoom the scale to hundredths and write every value as a decimal before you place it.

Q2 · Fill in the missing step (open answer: type the missing number; the index form is in the feedback)

Fill in the missing number, then write 84 as a product of prime factors in index form.

84 = 2 × 42 = 2 × 2 × ☐ = 2 × 2 × 3 × 7

Type the missing number in the box.

Hint (open it only after you have chosen an approach)

Keep dividing by the smallest prime that goes in exactly, and stop only when every tile is prime.

Q3 · Multiple choice

A circle has radius 6 cm. Its area is A = πr². Which answer is the exact area?

Hint (open it only after you have chosen an approach)

Treat π like a label, as you would “cm”, and do not swap it for a decimal.

Q4 · Numeric answer

Priya's bank balance is £42.60. A debit of £57.85 is taken from her account. Then a credit of £20.00 is paid in. What is her balance now?

Type the amount in pounds, without the £ sign (for example 1.50).

Hint (open it only after you have chosen an approach)

Draw a pin at £42.60 and move it left for money out, right for money in.
