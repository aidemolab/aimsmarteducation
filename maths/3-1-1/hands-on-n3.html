Q2 · Ordering or sequencing (shown as multiple choice: pick the right order)

You are told that 3⁴ = 81 and √196 = 14. Which order of steps works out 100 − 3⁴ ÷ 9 + √196 × 2?

A  81 ÷ 9 = 9
B  14 × 2 = 28
C  Replace 3⁴ with 81 and √196 with 14
D  100 − 9 = 91
E  91 + 28 = 119
