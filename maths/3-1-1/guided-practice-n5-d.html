Foundation replacements for N5.3 (everyone)

These replace Q7 to Q9 if you are taking Foundation. They practise listing.

Q10 · Numeric answer · Foundation replacement for Q7

A café offers 2 starters (soup, salad) and 3 mains (pasta, curry, fish). A meal is one starter and one main. List all the meals, holding the starter fixed. How many are there?

Hint 1

Start the first row with soup and go through all three mains.

Hint 2

Then do the same for salad.

Q11 · Matching (shown as multiple choice: pick the correct matching)

Match each situation to its number of outcomes (4, 6 or 12). (a) Throw a coin and roll an ordinary dice numbered 1 to 6. (b) Throw two coins, a 1p and a 2p. (c) Spin a spinner numbered 1 to 3 and throw a coin.

Hint 1

For (a), hold the coin fixed: heads with 1 to 6 is one row.

Hint 2

Count the slots in each row, then add the rows.

Q12 · Numeric answer · Foundation replacement for Q9 · no hints

List all the two-digit numbers you can make from the digits 1, 4 and 7, using each digit at most once. How many are there?
