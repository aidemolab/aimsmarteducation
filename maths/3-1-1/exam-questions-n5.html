Exam-style questions: N5 only

Original questions in the style of an AQA GCSE paper. Command words: list, write down, work out, show that, explain. M marks are for a correct method, A marks for a correct answer after a correct method, B marks for a correct answer or statement on its own.

Q1 · Numeric answer · [2 marks]

A fair coin is flipped and a fair four-sided spinner numbered 1, 2, 3 and 4 is spun. List all the possible outcomes on paper. Write down how many outcomes there are.

Mark scheme

M1 for a systematic list with at least one full row, for example holding heads fixed: H1, H2, H3, H4.

A1 for all 8 outcomes with none repeated.

Q2(a) · Numeric answer · [2 marks]

Leah has three cards: 2, 6 and 9. She uses two of the cards to make a two-digit number. (a) List all the two-digit numbers Leah can make on paper, then type how many there are.

Mark scheme

M1 for holding the tens digit fixed and listing at least one full row, for example 26, 29.

A1 for all six numbers (26, 29, 62, 69, 92, 96), with no repeats and no numbers such as 22 that use a card twice.

Q2(b) · Numeric answer · [1 mark]

(b) How many of these numbers are greater than 50?

Mark scheme

B1 for 4.

Q3 · Spot the error in a worked solution · [3 marks]

An ice-cream shop has 5 flavours. A customer chooses 2 different flavours for a cup. The order of the scoops does not matter. Zara's working: “There are 5 choices for the first scoop and 4 for the second, so there are 5 × 4 = 20 different cups.” What is the error, and what is the correct number of cups?

Mark scheme

B1 for explaining that each pair has been counted twice because the order does not matter.

M1 for dividing by 2, or for a systematic list.

A1 for 10.
