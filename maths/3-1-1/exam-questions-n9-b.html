Exam-style questions: N9 only

Original questions in the style of an AQA GCSE paper, never copied from AQA papers: 9 questions, 19 marks. M marks are for a correct method, A marks for a correct answer after a correct method, B marks for a correct answer or statement on its own. Open the mark scheme after you answer.

Q4 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: the answer is in standard form) · Non-calculator · [3 marks]

Work out 0.00025 ÷ 800. Give your answer in standard form.

Mark scheme

M1 for writing both numbers in standard form, 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ and 8 × 10².

M1 for dividing the parts, 2.5 ÷ 8 = 0.3125 and 10⁻⁴ ÷ 10² = 10⁻⁶, or for 0.3125 × 10⁻⁶ seen.

A1 for 3.125 × 10⁻⁷.

Q5 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: the answer is in standard form) · Non-calculator · [2 marks]

Work out (4 × 10⁷) × (6 × 10⁻³). Give your answer in standard form.

Mark scheme

M1 for 24 × 10⁴, or 240 000, seen.

A1 for 2.4 × 10⁵.

Q6 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: the answer is in standard form) · Non-calculator · [2 marks]

A city uses 2 × 10¹² litres of water in a year. It has 5 × 10⁶ people. Work out the mean number of litres used per person. Give your answer in standard form.

Mark scheme

M1 for (2 × 10¹²) ÷ (5 × 10⁶), or for 0.4 × 10⁶ seen.

A1 for 4 × 10⁵.
