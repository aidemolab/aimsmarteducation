Exam-style questions: N3 only

Original questions in the style of an AQA GCSE paper. Do them without a calculator. M marks are for a correct method, A marks for a correct answer after a correct method, B marks for a correct answer or statement on its own.

Q1 · Multiple choice · [1 mark]

Work out 12 − 8 ÷ 4 + 1

Mark scheme

B1 for 11.

8 ÷ 4 = 2 first; then left to right: 12 − 2 + 1 = 11.

Q2 · Numeric answer · [2 marks]

Work out (5 + 7) ÷ 2² × 3

Mark scheme

M1 for 12 ÷ 4 (= 3): the bracket and the power done first.

A1 for 9 (3 × 3, left to right).

Q3 · True or false with a reason · [2 marks]

Sam says, “(−5)² − 5² and −5² − 5² have the same value.” Is Sam correct?

Mark scheme

Command: you must show your working.

M1 for (−5)² = 25 and −5² = −25.

A1 for 0 and −50 with “No” or “False”.

Q4(a) · Numeric answer · [1 mark]

(a) Write down the reciprocal of 0.4. Type your answer as a decimal.

Mark scheme

B1 for 5/2 or 2.5.

Q4(b) · Open-ended (shown as multiple choice: pick the explanation that scores) · [1 mark]

(b) Explain why 0 has no reciprocal.

Mark scheme

B1 for an explanation that says 0 × any number = 0, so nothing multiplies by 0 to make 1.

“You can't divide by 0” on its own does not score.

Q4(c) · Numeric answer · [2 marks]

(c) Work out 9 − 6 ÷ ½

Mark scheme

This part uses N2 fraction division as its setting; the marks are for the N3 decisions.

M1 for 6 ÷ ½ = 12 (dividing by ½ = multiplying by its reciprocal, 2).

A1 for −3.
