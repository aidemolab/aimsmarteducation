Goal N9.3: Multiply or divide numbers in standard form

Worked example (skip it if you're confident)

(2 × 10³) × (3 × 10⁴) = (2 × 3) × (10³ × 10⁴) = 6 × 10⁷.

We can regroup because every step is multiplying, and we add the indices because 10³ × 10⁴ = 10⁷.

Q8 · Fill in the missing step

Fill in the missing index: (4 × 10³) × (2 × 10⁵) = (4 × 2) × (10³ × 10⁵) = 8 × 10^□

Hint 1

Every step is multiplying, so we can multiply the 4 and the 2, then the powers of ten.

Hint 2

10³ × 10⁵ is three tens multiplied, then five more tens multiplied. How many tens altogether?

Q9 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: the answer is in standard form)

Work out (6 × 10⁸) ÷ (3 × 10²). Give your answer in standard form.

Hint 1

Dividing by (3 × 10²) is the same as dividing by 3, then dividing by 10².

Hint 2

6 ÷ 3 = 2. For the powers of ten, subtract the indices.

Hint 3

8 − 2 = 6.

Q10 · Multiple choice · no hints

Work out (5 × 10⁴) × (6 × 10³). Which answer is in standard form?
