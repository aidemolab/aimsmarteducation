Q6 · Drag and drop into blanks (shown as multiple choice: pick the pair of cards for ☐ × 10^☐)

Write 0.00062 in standard form. Which two cards fill the blanks ☐ × 10^☐? Cards: 6.2, 62, −4, −3, 4

Hint (open it only after you have chosen an approach)

Fix A between 1 and 10 first, then count how many columns the digits must move.

Q7 · Spot the error in a worked solution (shown as multiple choice: pick the first false step and the correct answer)

Ben works out 20 − 12 ÷ 4 × 2 like this. Each step rewrites the whole line. Which step is the first one that is not true, and what is the correct answer?

Step 1: 20 − 12 ÷ 4 × 2 = 20 − 12 ÷ 8

Step 2: 20 − 12 ÷ 8 = 20 − 1.5

Step 3: 20 − 1.5 = 18.5

Hint (open it only after you have chosen an approach)

Draw a highlight box round each part that must collapse first, then check whether each line keeps the same value as the line before.

Q8 · Numeric answer

A café sells vanilla (V), chocolate (C) and strawberry (S) ice cream. Maya picks two different flavours in one cup, so V with C is the same cup as C with V. List every possible cup. How many are there?

List the cups on paper, then type how many there are.

Hint (open it only after you have chosen an approach)

Hold one flavour fixed, list all its partners, then move on and never go back.
