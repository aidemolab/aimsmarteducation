Goal N3.3: Find the reciprocal of a non-zero number

Worked example (skip it if you're confident)

Find the reciprocal of 5. The reciprocal is the number you multiply by to get 1. 1 ÷ 5 = 1/5, and 5 × 1/5 = 1. So the reciprocal of 5 is 1/5.

Q7 · Multiple choice

What is the reciprocal of 7?

Hint 1

The reciprocal is the number you multiply 7 by to get 1.

Hint 2

Work out 1 ÷ 7.
