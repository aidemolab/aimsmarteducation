HT Q13 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: the answer contains a surd) · Non-calculator · 3 marks

Rationalise the denominator of 4/(3 − √5). Give your answer in its simplest form.

Higher tier only. Foundation students can skip this part: Q21 is your question for this N point.

Mark scheme

M1 for multiplying the numerator and denominator by (3 + √5).

M1 for the denominator 9 − 5 = 4.

A1 for 3 + √5.

HT Q14 · Numeric answer · Non-calculator · 2 marks

How many four-digit whole numbers have four different digits? (A four-digit number cannot start with 0.)

Higher tier only. Foundation students can skip this part: Q22 is your question for this N point.

Mark scheme

M1 for 9 × 9 × 8 × 7.

A1 for 4536.

Q15 · True or false with a reason (shown as multiple choice: pick the verdicts; write the reasons on paper) · Non-calculator · 2 marks

For each statement, write true or false. Give a reason for each.

(a) −4 > −9

(b) 0.075 > 0.08

Mark scheme

B1 for (a) true with a reason (for example, −4 is to the right of −9 on a number line).

B1 for (b) false with a reason (for example, 0.080 is greater than 0.075).
