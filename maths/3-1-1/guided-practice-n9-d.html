Goal N9.4: Add or subtract numbers in standard form by first matching their powers of ten

Worked example (skip it if you're confident)

2 × 10⁴ + 3 × 10³.

Rewrite 2 × 10⁴ as 20 × 10³ (same pin).

Then 20 × 10³ + 3 × 10³ = 23 × 10³ = 2.3 × 10⁴.

Q11 · Fill in the missing step

Fill in the missing number to work out 4 × 10⁵ + 3 × 10⁴. 4 × 10⁵ = □ × 10⁴, so 4 × 10⁵ + 3 × 10⁴ = □ × 10⁴ + 3 × 10⁴ = 43 × 10⁴ = 4.3 × 10⁵.

Hint 1

To use 10⁴ instead of 10⁵, the power goes down by one. What must happen to the coefficient so that the value stays the same?

Hint 2

The coefficient must be ten times bigger: 4 × 10 = ?

Q12 · True or false with a reason

True or false? 2 × 10³ + 5 × 10³ = 7 × 10⁶. Choose a reason.

Hint 1

Write both numbers as ordinary numbers: 2000 and 5000. What is the total?

Hint 2

7000 in standard form is 7 × 10 to which power?

Q13 · Numeric answer (shown as multiple choice: the answer is in standard form) · no hints

Work out 9 × 10⁻³ − 6 × 10⁻⁴. Give your answer in standard form.
