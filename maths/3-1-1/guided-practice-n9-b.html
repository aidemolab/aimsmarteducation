Goal N9.2: Interpret a calculator's standard-form display

Worked example (skip it if you're confident)

A calculator shows 5.1E06.

The E means “× 10 to the power”, so this is 5.1 × 10⁶ = 5 100 000.

Q5 · Multiple choice

A calculator display shows 3.5E−06. What does it mean?

Hint 1

What does the letter E stand for on a calculator display?

Hint 2

E means “× 10 to the power”. Now move the digits of 3.5 six columns to the right.

Q6 · Matching (shown as multiple choice: pick the correct matching)

Match each calculator display to its ordinary number. Displays: 2.7E04 · 2.7E−04 · 7.2E02 · 7.2E−02. Numbers: 720 · 0.072 · 27 000 · 0.00027.

Hint 1

First sort the displays: a positive power gives a number bigger than 1; a negative power gives a number less than 1.

Hint 2

Then count columns: the power tells you how many columns the digits move.

Q7 · Numeric answer · no hints

You work out 0.0008 × 0.0003 on a calculator. The display shows 2.4E−07. Write the answer as an ordinary number.
