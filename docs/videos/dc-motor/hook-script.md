# Hook video: "What pushes the blades round?"

Draft for Alaa's review. Module: How a DC motor works (AQA GCSE Physics 8463 v1.1, 4.7.2).

- **Learning objective:** raise the puzzle and get a prediction; no teaching yet.
- **Audience:** GCSE Physics, Foundation and Higher (the puzzle leads into 4.7.2.1 for everyone).
- **Core idea (one sentence):** a fan spins with no engine and no fuel, so something else must push it.
- **Length target:** 20–30 seconds. 53 words at about 2.3 words per second ≈ 23 s, plus pauses.
- **Tool:** HyperFrames (visuals), AIM Narrator (ElevenLabs), FFmpeg.
- **Sits on:** `physics/dc-motor/hook.html`, above the warm-up and the first prediction.
- **Pause point:** at the end, the page shows "Make your prediction" and links to the prediction below. Nothing is explained.

## Narration (one take)

> Flick a switch, and a fan starts spinning straight away. There's no engine inside. No fuel. Just a coil of wire, a couple of magnets, and an electric current. So what is actually pushing the blades round? Before we explain anything, make a prediction. Lock it in. Then test it in the model.
